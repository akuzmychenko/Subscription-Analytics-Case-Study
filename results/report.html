Findings

  1. 2025-01 to 2025-12: registrations 215 → 600; mature 7-day conversion 26.51% → 20.36% (-6.15 percentage points).
  2. Collected cash changed from EUR 579.49 in 2025-01 to EUR 5380.36 in 2025-12. This includes renewals, so it does not measure new-user conversion alone.
  3. Referral has the highest observed channel conversion (36.12%); Social has the lowest (11.46%). These are scenario-specific associations, not causal marketing effects.
  4. 47.44% of January registrants returned to view content in February. This measures activity, not paid renewal.

Interpretation and next steps

Growth and conversion are different questions

Evidence. Registrations changed from 215 to 600 between 2025-01 and 2025-12. Mature 7-day conversion changed from 26.51% to 20.36%: -6.15 percentage points.

Registrations measure acquisition volume. Conversion measures how often sufficiently observed new users make a first successful payment. More registrations do not automatically mean a stronger conversion rate.

Next check. Compare conversion within signup month, country and acquisition channel. Use counts together with rates; do not average channel percentages without their denominators.

Boundary. The monthly endpoints are a descriptive comparison, not a trend-significance test or a causal result.

Separate cash from new-customer acquisition

Evidence. First successful payments contributed EUR 12868.81; subsequent successful payments contributed EUR 25448.18 (66.41% of collected cash). Monthly cash changed from EUR 579.49 to EUR 5380.36.

Cash includes payments from the accumulated audience. The first-versus-renewal split shows why total cash and new-user conversion need not move together. It does not by itself attribute the entire monthly change to renewals.

Next check. Use SQL question 12 to verify the split. To explain monthly growth further, break first-payment and renewal cash down by payment month.

Boundary. Cash excludes failed attempts and is not profit, MRR, accrual revenue or a renewal probability.

Channel differences need context

Evidence. Referral converted 259 of 717 eligible users (36.12%), versus 173 of 1509 for Social (11.46%). The difference is 24.66 percentage points.

This describes the acquisition mix in the current dataset. It identifies a comparison worth investigating; it does not establish that shifting budget to the highest-rate channel will improve outcomes.

Next check. Inspect country and signup-month composition and obtain acquisition spend before considering budget changes.

Boundary. Channel assignment is not randomized, and channel quality differs by construction in the generator. Individual 95% intervals are not a multiple-comparison or causal test.

Read retention as return-to-content activity

Evidence. 102 of 215 January registrants returned in February: 47.44% calendar-month M1 activity retention.

The cohort denominator includes everyone who registered in that month, including non-payers. A return event means at least one content view; it says nothing about daily frequency or paid renewal.

Next check. Compare cohorts at the same month offset. On real data, validate events and compare users with similar follow-up before recommending engagement changes.

Boundary. M0 is 100% because of signup-day events. Late-month signups have shorter first-month exposure. Unobserved future cells are NULL, not zero.

12. How much collected cash comes from first payments versus renewals?
WITH successful AS (
    SELECT s.user_id, p.payment_id, p.payment_date, p.amount_cents,
           ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
               PARTITION BY s.user_id ORDER BY p.payment_date, p.payment_id
           ) AS payment_number
    FROM payments p JOIN subscriptions s USING(subscription_id)
    WHERE p.status = 'succeeded'
), classified AS (
    SELECT CASE WHEN payment_number = 1 THEN 'First payment' ELSE 'Renewal' END AS payment_type,
           amount_cents
    FROM successful
)
SELECT payment_type, COUNT(*) AS successful_payments,
       SUM(amount_cents) / 100.0 AS cash_eur,
       1.0 * SUM(amount_cents) / NULLIF((SELECT SUM(amount_cents) FROM successful), 0) AS cash_share
FROM classified
GROUP BY payment_type
ORDER BY payment_type;

Metric boundaries

  • Conversion includes signup day and the following six dates. Users without all seven dates are excluded.
  • Cash excludes failed attempts. It is not profit, MRR or accrual revenue.
  • Retention means at least one content view in a calendar month, not paid subscription renewal.
  • M0 is 100% because every synthetic user has a signup-day event. Grey future cells are unobserved, not zero.
  • Channel error bars are individual 95% score intervals. Differences are observational and reflect generation assumptions.
